Can you recommend 6 laptops that would suit my needs? For each laptop, please provide high details about its price, processor, RAM, storage, display, and battery life (without pictures).

Here are six laptops that suit work, study, assignments and presentations, browsing and online classes. Prices are US list prices in USD, so local prices will differ.

1. Apple MacBook Air 13-inch (M5)

  • Price: $1,099 list; about $900 on sale at Amazon; education pricing can lower it further.
  • Processor: Apple M5, 10-core CPU, up to 10-core GPU.
  • RAM: 16GB base, configurable up to 32GB; soldered, so it can’t be upgraded later.
  • Storage: 512GB base, up to 4TB.
  • Display: 13.6-inch Liquid Retina, 2560 x 1664.
  • Battery: up to 18 hours of video or about 15 hours of mixed productivity use; weighs 2.7 lb.
  • Notes: Wi-Fi 7, two Thunderbolt 4 ports and MagSafe charging.

2. Apple MacBook Neo

  • Price: $599 (256GB), or $699 with 512GB and Touch ID; education price $499.
  • Processor: A18 Pro with a 6-core CPU.
  • RAM: 8GB, not upgradeable.
  • Storage: 256GB or 512GB SSD.
  • Display: 13-inch Liquid Retina, 2408 x 1506, up to 500 nits.
  • Battery: 36.5 Wh; up to 16 hours of video or about 11 hours of web browsing; weighs 2.7 lb.
  • Notes: no Thunderbolt, no keyboard backlight, and Touch ID only on the higher storage model.

3. Dell XPS 13 (2026)

  • Price: from $699, or $599 for eligible students.
  • Processor: Intel Core 5 320 or Core Ultra 7 355.
  • RAM: up to 32GB; the Core 5 320 version lists 16GB DDR5.
  • Storage: up to 1TB.
  • Display: 13.4-inch 2560 x 1600 touchscreen, 500 nits, full DCI-P3 colour, 120Hz refresh rate that can drop to 30Hz to save battery.
  • Battery: 65Wh; Dell claims up to 17 hours of 4K streaming; weighs 2.2 pounds.
  • Notes: touchscreen and backlit keyboard; Windows compatibility.

4. Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 7

  • Price: from about $782 direct from Lenovo; a Ryzen 7 250, 16GB, 512GB model was listed near $979.
  • Processor: AMD Ryzen 7 250, Intel Core Ultra 7 255H, Core Ultra 5 228V, or Core 7 240H.
  • RAM: 16GB DDR5 is typical; 32GB versions are sold. On the Lunar Lake version the RAM can’t be upgraded.
  • Storage: 512GB NVMe SSD is typical; 1TB variants exist.
  • Display: 14-inch 1920 x 1200 IPS as standard; the Lunar Lake model has 2880 x 1800; an optional 120Hz display exists.
  • Battery: 48Wh or 64Wh; Lenovo cites up to 14.75 hours in its best-battery configuration; weighs about 1.34 kg (2.95 lb).
  • Notes: Thunderbolt 4 ports and a well-regarded keyboard. Battery results vary a lot by configuration.

5. Acer Aspire 14 AI

  • Price: the model CNET reviewed was $700, on sale for $500; deals as low as $460 (down from $830) have appeared.
  • Processor: Intel Core Ultra 5 226V; pricier versions use Core Ultra 7 256V.
  • RAM: 16GB LPDDR5X; some versions have 32GB.
  • Storage: 512GB on cheaper models, 1TB on others.
  • Display: 14-inch 1920 x 1200 touch IPS; an OLED version also exists.
  • Battery: nearly 19 hours in CNET’s YouTube streaming test; weighs about 1.4 kg.
  • Notes: reviewers criticise its dull display and design.

6. ASUS Zenbook A14 (2026)

  • Price: starts around $1,099; a 16GB/512GB model was listed at $1,349.99 at Best Buy.
  • Processor: Snapdragon X2 Elite, 18 cores.
  • RAM: 16GB LPDDR5X in the base model; some versions have 32GB.
  • Storage: 512GB SSD base, with a 1TB option.
  • Display: 14-inch 1920 x 1200 OLED, limited to 60Hz.
  • Battery: 70Wh; one reviewer’s test lasted nearly 30 hours; weighs about 0.99 kg (2.18 lb).
  • Notes: runs Windows on Arm, and reviewers point to remaining compatibility limitations.
